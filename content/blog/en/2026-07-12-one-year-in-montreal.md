Already a whole year living in Québec's metropolis! It's fascinating how fast
time goes by. To say that one year ago I decided (a little bit on a whim lol)
to look to Montréal for a new place to live.

In retrospect, I can confidently say this was the right choice! I've had the
privilege of living in and discovering an amazing city. From the old port to the
Jean-Talon market, passing by the Olympic Park, the Lachine Canal and the
La Fontaine park. Without forgetting the Bixis, the bike paths, the metro and
the REM. I really admire the culture melting pot that is this city (even though
I don't feel like I participate in it due to being chronically online on my
computer). I fell in love with this island and I'm proud to call it my home.

## What Started It All

After a series of events, I ended up having to find a new apartment in the
middle of June 2025. In case you're not aware, in Québec, the vast majority of
leases start and end on July 1st. This gave me very *very* little leeway for
finding a new place to live.

Despite that, a small idea appeared in the back of my mind a few weeks prior:
"What if I moved to Montréal?" In fact, at the start of June 2025, I was
touring the city as part of a mini vacation. I remember having this thought as
I was strolling through the breathtaking roads of the plateau. A few reasons
then pushed me to consider this decision more seriously.

First, I had been living in the city of Québec for four years at that point and
it was clear I loved the city life. It was then evident for me that I had to try
the Montréal life, even if it were for a single year.

Second, while living in Québec City, I developed an interest for urbanism and
sustainable mobility. I knew that Montréal would serve me a lot better in this
department with its multiple protected bike paths plus its bus and metro
network. Enough even to allow me to live car free. I must say that not owning
a car and not having to manage maintenance were very enticing. Especially since
I was already getting around using mostly public transit.

It was settled. I was going to search for an apartment on the Montréal Island!

## Initial Adaptation

Thankfully for me, everything went well and I managed to find a place to stay
in a nice neighbourhood, all within my tight deadline!

The first thing I loved was how close by essential services were. Contrary to
my old place, everything I needed in my day-to-day life was available within
a fifteen minutes walk! Combined with the metro system that allows me to reach
downtown in a timely manner, I knew immediately I had made the right decision.

One thing I loved doing when I was still unfamiliar with the city was to take
a bus or metro, get out at a random stop, stroll around different neighbourhoods
and try whatever restaurant was there. I was simultaneously getting to know
the city and eating good food. Two birds one stone :D

## Becoming Vegan

It was during one of these little outings on the plateau that I met an activist
group fighting for animal rights. After talking with them, they convinced me
to be vegan. Be it on an environmental, ethic or health level, this lifestyle
matches my values and it's now a part of my daily life. The switch was not that
hard for me since I already stopped consuming meat about two months prior.

I discovered that there are a lot of great vegan restaurants, or even just
normal restaurants with tasty vegan options! My personal favourite is ZAC
pizzeria which is located between the Mont-Royal and the Laurier stations.
Their food is indescribably delicious! By far the best pizza in my opinion. If
you are around, I can strongly recommend it and it is well worth the detour if
you are around!

## Encounters, Travels and Discoveries

If there was a theme that stuck around for the past year, it was discovery.

It started with the discovery of new people. Namely, by being more active online
and no Bluesky, I met and became friends with many incredible people and creatures.
A quiet reminder that a few simple social interactions can lead to amazing
outcomes!

These new friends then pushed me to discover new places. I'm talking in part
about an express four-day trip I took to Belgium to attend FOSDEM. Even though
my time there was short, I absolutely loved meeting friends IRL and doing an
expedited visit of Europe. It really opened my eyes on the number of things
there are to see and experience in this world. I can't wait to have the
opportunity to go back there and visit a lot of new places while pacing myself
properly this time.

## Conclusion

Finally, all these events made me discover a little bit more about who I am.
Discover that social relationships are a lot more valuable than what I gave them
credit for. Discover that I still have so much of this universe to discover.

Overall, I am very satisfied with the direction my life took within the last
year and I want to keep going that way.
